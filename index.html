What you can do

A compact, stylised open world built for mobile play.

🏙️

Free-roam open world

Dynamic traffic and pedestrians across a 512×512 metre coastal district with 130 buildings.

🚔

Police pursuit

A heat system that reacts to what you do — break line of sight, or take the arrest.

🚗

Five vehicle types

Steal, drive, and crash your way across the city. Vehicle-to-vehicle damage included.

👊

Punches & a sidearm

A 12-round sidearm with held fire and automatic reload, plus hand-to-hand combat.

📜

Four story contracts

Authored, replayable missions with local progress, then continued free roaming.

📴

Fully offline

No account, no login, no internet connection required. Progress saves to your device only.

About Hood City

Hood City is developed by AnIntellectualCompany. It's a compact, stylised open-world action, driving and courier game — not a AAA production, built for offline mobile play with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no data collection.
