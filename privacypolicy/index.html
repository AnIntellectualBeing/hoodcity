Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Hood City ("the app") is developed by AnIntellectualCompany ("we", "us"). This page explains how the app handles data.

Data collection

Hood City is a fully offline, single-player game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or usage analytics. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

Local storage only

Game progress (credits, completed jobs, best times, and settings) is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Hood City contains no advertising and no third-party advertising SDKs.

In-app purchases

Hood City contains no in-app purchases and no payment processing.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions — no internet, storage, microphone, location, or contacts access.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because the app collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to anintellectualcompany@gmail.com.
